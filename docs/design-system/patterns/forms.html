Overview

Gattsby has few forms: add a note or location, correct what was read from a photo, import, and type “delete” to delete all data. Fields are full width, labels above, help below, errors under the field and summarised at the top.

When to use

  • Use a form only for what cannot be picked: names, notes, corrections.
  • Prefer quick replies and buttons in the investigation.

States

StateRule
DefaultNothing is validated before the first submit.
InvalidOn submit: summary at the top with links, focus moves to it; each field shows its error; re-validate on input.
BusyThe primary shows loading; fields stay editable.
FailedA danger alert says nothing was lost and offers Retry.

Responsive behaviour

  • One column at every width; sheets cap at 28rem.

Accessibility

  • Labels visible; optional marked; aria-invalid and aria-describedby on errors.
  • The summary is role="alert" with tabindex="-1".
  • autocomplete="off" on location: Gattsby never looks up where the person is.

Content

  • Errors say how to fix it.
  • Destructive confirmation by typing a word (“delete”) for delete all data (FR-065).
