Feedback and loading

Overview

Choose feedback by where the person is looking and what they must do.

When to use

SituationUse
A field is wrongInline error + summary
Something about this screen changed (connection, mode)Banner above the feed
Something finished elsewhere (export saved)Toast
A decision is needed before going onSheet
A page is loading from storageSkeleton
A named task of unknown lengthSpinner with text
A task of known sizeProgress bar

Structure

Connection dropped 6 s into the recording.

Reconnecting, attempt 2 of 3. I’ll repeat this step and ignore the partial data.

Saved horizon-t202-2026-11-25-profile.gattsby.json
It’s in your Downloads folder. Nothing was sent anywhere.
Banner and toast.

States

StateRule
LoadingSkeleton for up to 10 s, then the error state.
ReconnectingWarning banner with spinner and attempt count (NFR-007).
RecoveredSuccess banner, then it clears.
FailedDanger banner with Try again; nothing found so far is lost.

Responsive behaviour

  • Banners sit above the feed and never scroll away.
  • Toasts never cover the pinned primary action.

Accessibility

  • Banners are role="status"; failures needing action are role="alert".
  • Spinners have hidden text naming the task.

Content

  • Say what Gattsby is doing about it.
  • Say nothing was lost when that is true.
