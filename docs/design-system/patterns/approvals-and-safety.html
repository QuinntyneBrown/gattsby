Pattern

Approvals and safety

Approval sheets, the stand-clear reminder, lowest risk first, the emergency stop.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview
  • When to use
  • Structure
  • States
  • Responsive behaviour
  • Accessibility
  • Content
  • Do and don't
  • Sources

Overview

Gattsby is read-only by default. Every write or control tool call stops in an approval sheet, every time, and goes only when the person says yes to that one call (FR-034, NFR-018). Before any motion command the sheet reminds the person to stand clear and to know where the machine’s own stop is (FR-035). The emergency stop is one tap away on every connected screen and inside every sheet (FR-039).

These rules outrank everything else in this system: no setting, mode, layout or component may weaken them.

When to use

  • An approval sheet for each write or control call, never batched, including a vendor handshake that moves nothing (FR-032).
  • The stand-clear callout and its checkbox for any call that can move the machine; a call that moves nothing says so in its risk row and has no tick, and focus starts on Don’t send.
  • The emergency stop in the app bar and every sheet’s toolbar whenever a device is connected.
  • The emergency-stop result sheet after a press; the unexpected-effect sheet when the machine did something other than expected.

Structure

Emergency stop

Command 2 of 3 · needs your approval

Lower the target speed to 5.9 km/h?

I’ll send “set target speed” to the control point. The belt should ease from 6.0 to 5.9 km/h, the smallest step it supports. Then I’ll ask whether it did, and check the data.

Stand clear of the back of the belt and keep the safety key clipped on. Know where the machine’s own stop is — and the red Emergency stop here.

Responsive behaviour

  • Bottom sheet on phones with the footer pinned; the tick and Send are one thumb movement apart.
  • The stop stays in the sheet toolbar at every width.

Accessibility

  • Focus starts on the checkbox so the reminder is read first.
  • Esc and Close never send.
  • The emergency-stop label holds 7:1.
  • Send’s disabled reason is visible text, not only a disabled state.

Content

  • Lowest risk first: change one value by the smallest step the machine supports (FR-036); never a high speed or incline.
  • Name the risk in words: “Low — slows the belt by 0.1 km/h”, “None — asks for status, moves nothing”.
  • Say where a handshake came from: “Knowledge base 2026.11 · Kingsmith signature”.
  • After sending, ask what the person saw before the next command: “Does the console show 5.9 km/h?”
