App bar

The app bar holds Back, the device or page title with its mode and stage, the options and, while a device is connected, the emergency stop. It is sticky at the top of every screen.

Overview

Two shapes: the investigation bar (Back, title and subtitle, Pause, Options, Emergency stop) and the home bar (the brand and the readiness/settings actions). Titles truncate with an ellipsis; actions never do.

Anatomy

  1. BackGhost icon button; leaving asks first (FR-018).
  2. TitleDevice name, --text-h3.
  3. SubtitleMode · stage.
  4. ActionsPause (hidden under 768px), Options.
  5. Emergency stopWhile connected.

Sizes

One height: 64px (<code>--layout-topbar-height</code>).

States

The bar has no states of its own; its buttons do. Two content states matter:

  • Connected: the emergency stop shows.
  • Not connected (verdict, saved devices): no stop; the actions are Export and Options.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 768px: Back, title, Options and Emergency stop; Pause moves into the options sheet; the stop label shortens to “Stop”.
  • From 768px the side padding grows to 16px and Pause returns.
  • Sticky at every width; under 768px that keeps the emergency stop visible while scrolling (NFR-010 AC2).

Theming

The bar uses --color-bg-canvas with a --color-border-default rule, so it blends with the page and the stop is the only colour in it.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<header class="appbar"> landmark; the title is the page’s <h1>.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabBack, then each action in order, ending with the emergency stop.

Focus

The shared ring (--focus-ring-width 2px of --color-focus-ring, 2px offset) appears on :focus-visible only, so a tap never leaves a ring behind.

Labelling

Icon buttons carry names that say the consequence: “Leave the investigation”, “Investigation options”, “Pause”.

Contrast

Live ratios, light then dark.

Title
Subtitle

Motion

None; it does not hide on scroll.

Touch

All actions 48px; at least 4px apart, with the stop separated from Options by the gap.

Content

  • Title: the device’s name as the person would say it (“Horizon T202”), or the advertised name until it is known (“HZN-T202-4F1A”).
  • Subtitle: “Agent · Standard path” or “Checklist · Identify”.
