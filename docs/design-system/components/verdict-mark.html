Verdict mark

The verdict mark is a small shape that says how an investigation ended: connected, partially decoded, needs a missing tool, not connectable or stopped. It always sits beside the verdict in words.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Five shapes, each paired with the verdict words (FR-054): filled green disc Connected, half-filled amber ring Partially decoded, signal ring Needs a missing tool, muted cross Not connectable, muted square Stopped by the person.
