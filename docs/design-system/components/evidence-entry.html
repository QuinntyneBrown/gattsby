Evidence entry

Evidence entries are the raw investigation record: every packet sent and received, every answer and every agent step, with time, direction, characteristic, bytes and the decoded meaning.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableFR-056

Overview

The evidence log (.ev-list) is the audit trail behind every observation. Each .ev has a time, a direction arrow (→ sent, ← received, • person or agent), the characteristic, the bytes in mono and a plain reading. An open entry shows the decode table.

Anatomy

  1. Timehh:mm:ss, mono.
  2. Direction→ sent (signal), ← received (success), • person/agent.
  3. CharacteristicService / characteristic and operation.
  4. BytesMono hex.
  5. DecodeField → raw → value.

States

Entries are closed or open (.ev--open or aria-expanded="true" on the toggle). The log itself has default, filtered, no-results, empty, loading and error states: see the mocks.

Responsive behaviour

  • Time and arrow stay in fixed columns; bytes wrap anywhere.
  • The decode table indents 32px on phones and 92px from 576px to line up under the bytes.

Theming

Sent arrows read --color-fg-signal, received --color-success-fg; an open entry sits on --color-bg-surface.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An ordered list; each entry has visually hidden “Sent:”, “Received:”, “You:” or “Agent:”. Bytes and decoded strings from the device are rendered as text, never HTML (NFR-013).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to each entry’s toggle.
EnterOpens or closes the decode.

Focus

The shared ring (--focus-ring-width 2px of --color-focus-ring, 2px offset) appears on :focus-visible only, so a tap never leaves a ring behind.

Labelling

The arrow is aria-hidden; the hidden word carries direction.

Contrast

Live ratios, light then dark.
