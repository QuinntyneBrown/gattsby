Checkbox

A checkbox records a yes the person gives deliberately: that they are clear of the machine, or that photos go into an export.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Gattsby uses a checkbox where ticking it has a consequence the person must own. The approval sheet’s “I am clear and know where the stop is” must be ticked before Send enables (FR-035); the export sheet’s “Include photos” is unticked by default (FR-063). Use a switch for a setting that takes effect at once.

Anatomy

  1. Box24px, --radius-xs, ink fill and a paper tick when checked.
  2. Label16px body text; the whole row is the target.

Variants

Single and with a description.
HTML
<div class="stack"><label class="choice"><input type="checkbox" ><span>I am clear and know where the stop is</span></label><label class="choice"><input type="checkbox"><span class="choice__text">Include photos<small>3 photos · 2.1 MB. They stay out unless you tick this.</small></span></label></div>

Sizes

One size: a 24px box in a row at least 48px tall.

States

VariantUncheckedCheckedFocusDisabledInvalid
Checkbox

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

  • The label wraps under itself, never under the box.
  • In sheets the checkbox sits directly above the footer so the tick and Send are one thumb movement apart.

Theming

Unchecked reads --color-border-strong on --color-bg-surface; checked fills with --color-accent and draws the tick in --color-fg-on-accent, the same pair as the primary button.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <input type="checkbox"> inside its <label class="choice">.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the box.
SpaceToggles.

Focus

The shared ring (--focus-ring-width 2px of --color-focus-ring, 2px offset) appears on :focus-visible only, so a tap never leaves a ring behind. In the approval sheet the checkbox takes focus first, so the safety reminder is read before Send.

Labelling

The label text is the name. A description sits inside the label as .choice__text small.

Contrast

Live ratios, light then dark.

Motion

Fill eases over --duration-fast.

Touch

Row at least 48px tall; the label is part of the target.

Content

  • Write the label as what the person affirms, first person: “I am clear and know where the stop is”.
  • Never pre-tick a box that sends data or skips a safety step.
