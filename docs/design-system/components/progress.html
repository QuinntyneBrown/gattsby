Progress and meter

A progress bar shows how much of a task is done, such as the model download. A meter shows a level, such as the hypothesis confidence.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

  • Progress (.progress, role="progressbar"): determinate with a size readout, or indeterminate.
  • Meter (.meter): a 56px track and a mono percentage beside the hypothesis.

Anatomy

  1. LabelWhat is downloading.
  2. Readout“1.4 of 2.2 GB”, mono.
  3. Track and bar8px, full radius.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width of its container; the meter keeps a fixed 56px track except --lg.

Theming

Bars read --color-accent on a --color-bg-subtle-hover track; success and danger swap the bar.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow/min/max; the confidence meter uses role="meter" in the system (the mocks used progressbar).

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable; content is read in order.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

aria-label (“Model download”, “Confidence”); the visible readout repeats the value.

Contrast

Live ratios, light then dark.

Bar on track
Readout

Motion

Width eases over --duration-deliberate; indeterminate loops at --duration-shimmer and becomes a static full bar under reduced motion.

Touch

Not a target.

Content

  • Readouts in the units Chrome reports: “1.4 of 2.2 GB”.
  • Confidence always with the hypothesis in words beside it.
