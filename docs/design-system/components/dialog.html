Dialog

Dialogs are sheets: they rise from the bottom on phones and sit centred from 576px. Every sheet over a connected investigation carries the emergency stop in its toolbar.

Overview

Use a sheet for a decision that must happen before the investigation goes on: approving a command, leaving, stopping, exporting, importing, deleting. The page behind is inert. Variants: default, danger (red ring, emergency-stop result), form (add a note), and menu (options).

Anatomy

  1. GrabberPhones only; decorative.
  2. ToolbarEmergency stop and Close.
  3. HeaderEyebrow, title, lead.
  4. BodyCallouts, bytes, fields.
  5. FooterSticky; primary first on phones.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 576px: a bottom sheet, full width, top corners --radius-xl, max 88% of the viewport height, footer sticky and buttons stacked full width.
  • From 576px: centred with 24px around it, all corners rounded, footer buttons in one row.
  • The grabber shows only under 576px.

Theming

Panels use --color-bg-surface-raised and --shadow-4; in ink the raised surface is lighter than the canvas so the sheet still reads as above it. The danger ring is --color-border-danger.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA APG Dialog (Modal): role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-labelledby aria-describedby; the native <dialog> with showModal() in the product. The page behind is inert.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabCycles inside the sheet.
EscCloses, the same as Close or Don’t send; never sends.
EnterActivates the focused button only.

Focus

On open, focus moves to the first input (the stand-clear checkbox in approval) or the title; on close it returns to the trigger.

Labelling

Title is the accessible name; lead is the description. Close is “Close”.

Contrast

Live ratios, light then dark.

Motion

Slides up (phones) or scales in (from 576px) over --duration-slow with --ease-enter; the backdrop fades over --duration-base. Instant under reduced motion.

Touch

Footer buttons are 56px and full width on phones; Close and the stop are 48px.

Content

  • Title asks or states the decision: “Lower the target speed to 5.9 km/h?”, “Leave this investigation?”.
  • Lead says what will happen in plain words; the eyebrow gives context (“Command 2 of 3 · needs your approval”).
  • Buttons say the action and its opposite: Send / Don’t send.
