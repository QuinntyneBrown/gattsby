Brand

The mark is an open G drawn as a scan arc whose crossbar stops short of a dot: the unknown device. The wordmark is lower-case Geist with a tight “tt”.

Overview

The brand appears in the home app bar, on the readiness check (large) and as the agent’s avatar in the activity feed. The dot is the only signal-orange part of the mark.

Anatomy

  1. Arc7-unit round stroke in currentColor.
  2. Device dot--color-signal, r 4.5 of 64.
  3. WordmarkSemibold, −0.045em; “tt” at −0.11em.

Theming

The arc and wordmark follow --color-fg-default; the dot stays --color-signal in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A link home with aria-label="Gattsby, home"; the SVG and wordmark are aria-hidden.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the home link.
EnterGoes home.

Focus

The shared ring (--focus-ring-width 2px of --color-focus-ring, 2px offset) appears on :focus-visible only, so a tap never leaves a ring behind.

Labelling

The link label; never “logo”.

Contrast

Live ratios, light then dark.
