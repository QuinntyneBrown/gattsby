Readiness check

The readiness check lists six capabilities, each with a status word and a shape: browser, Bluetooth, on-device model, advertisement scanning, camera, storage.

Overview

.checks is a ruled list of .check rows: an icon in a tinted circle (data-status="ok|warn|fail|na|pending"), the capability, a detail line, and the status word on the right: Available, Needs action, Not available.

Anatomy

  1. Status iconTinted circle; tick, warning, cross, dash or spinner.
  2. Capability16px medium.
  3. DetailWhat was found.
  4. Status wordCaption.

Responsive behaviour

  • Single column; the status word stays on the right and the detail wraps.

Theming

Icons sit on status backgrounds (--color-success-bg, --color-warning-bg, --color-danger-bg, --color-bg-subtle) with matching foregrounds.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A labelled <ul>; icons are aria-hidden; the status word is text.
