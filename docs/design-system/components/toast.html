Toast

Toasts confirm something that happened outside the current view, such as an export saved to Downloads, or report a background failure with a way to retry.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Toasts are inverse cards at the bottom of the screen. Success and info dismiss themselves after 8 s and pause on hover or focus; warning and danger stay until dismissed. Never use a toast for a safety message or for something the person must decide: use a sheet.

Anatomy

  1. IconTone colour on inverse.
  2. TitleWhat happened.
  3. TextWhere it went or what to do.
  4. Dismiss48px.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 576px: full width, 16px from the edges, above the pinned action bar.
  • From 576px: 24rem wide, bottom right, 24px in.
  • Stacked toasts sit 12px apart, newest at the bottom.

Theming

Inverse in both themes (--color-bg-inverse); icons use the --color-*-on-inverse tones.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A role="status" region (polite) holds success and info; danger toasts use role="alert".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabAction, then Dismiss.
EscDismisses the focused toast.

Focus

The shared ring (--focus-ring-width 2px of --color-focus-ring, 2px offset) appears on :focus-visible only, so a tap never leaves a ring behind. Toasts never take focus.

Labelling

Dismiss is “Dismiss”.

Contrast

Live ratios, light then dark.

Success icon
Danger icon

Motion

Slides up over --duration-base; fades out on leave. Instant under reduced motion.

Touch

Action and Dismiss 48px; toasts never cover the pinned primary action.

Content

  • Title: the outcome with the object (“Saved horizon-t202-…-profile.gattsby.json”).
  • Say nothing was sent anywhere when that matters (NFR-012).
