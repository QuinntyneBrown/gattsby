Emergency stop

The emergency stop sends stop or reset, refuses every later write and stops the agent. It is one tap away on every screen while a device is connected, including inside every sheet.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableFR-039NFR-011: 7:1 label

Overview

This is the most important control in Gattsby. It is a pill-shaped danger button with a stop-sign icon, pinned in the app bar while a device is connected and repeated in the toolbar of every sheet that opens over a connected investigation, because the page behind a sheet is inert (FR-039, NFR-010 AC2).

  • Never hide it, move it into a menu or put it behind a confirmation. Pressing it acts at once; the sheet that follows reports the result.
  • It is not a destructive action in the usual sense and never uses .btn--danger: it has its own shape so it cannot be mistaken for Delete.
  • Disconnected, it disappears; while the stop is being sent, it shows as busy; once sent it disables and says so.

Anatomy

  1. Stop iconOctagon with a cross; never another icon.
  2. Label“Emergency stop”; under 768px it shows “Stop” and keeps “Emergency stop” for screen readers.
  3. ContainerDanger solid, full radius, --color-border-danger edge, semibold label, 48px tall.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 768px the label shortens to “Stop” to leave room for the device name; the accessible name stays “Emergency stop”.
  • Under 768px it stays pinned at the top in the sticky app bar while the person scrolls (NFR-010 AC2).
  • In sheets it rides in .dialog__toolbar, top right, beside Close; on phones the sheet’s grabber sits above it.

Theming

The fill is --color-danger-solid (#A11D1D) in both themes so the white label holds 7:1. On ink that red is only 2.2:1 against the canvas, so the edge uses --color-border-danger (red-300 in dark) to keep the shape findable.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An <a href> to the emergency-stop route in the mocks; in the product a <button type="button"> that acts at once. No ARIA role is needed.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches it right after the app bar’s other actions; in sheets it is the first focusable element after the grabber.
Enter, SpaceSends stop at once. There is no confirmation.

Focus

The shared ring (--focus-ring-width 2px of --color-focus-ring, 2px offset) appears on :focus-visible only, so a tap never leaves a ring behind. Focus never starts on the stop when a sheet opens; it starts on the sheet’s first input or primary action.

Labelling

“Emergency stop” in full. The short “Stop” is aria-hidden; the long label is visually hidden on phones, not removed.

Contrast

Live ratios, light then dark.

Motion

Hover and press as a button; nothing animates on its own. The busy spinner keeps turning under reduced motion.

Touch

48px tall, at least 8px from any neighbour; placed top right where a phone held in one hand still reaches it, and never adjacent to Delete.

Content

  • Always “Emergency stop”. On phones, “Stop” visually.
  • The result sheet says what happened and what to do with the machine: “Stop sent. The belt should be slowing. If it isn’t, use the machine’s own stop.”
  • Never “Abort”, “Kill” or “Cancel”.
