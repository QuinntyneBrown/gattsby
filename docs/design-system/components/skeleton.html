Skeleton

Skeletons hold the shape of a page while its content loads from storage, so nothing jumps when it arrives.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • Anatomy
  • Variants
  • Sizes

Overview

Shapes: text lines, a circle, a row, a card, a button, a feed bubble. The container sets aria-busy="true"; skeletons are aria-hidden.

Anatomy

  1. Title1.5em, 40% wide.
  2. Text0.9em lines; the last at 60%.

Variants

Title, text, row, card, button, bubble.
HTML
<div class="stack" style="width: 100%;"><span class="skeleton skeleton--title" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="skeleton skeleton--text" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="skeleton skeleton--row" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="skeleton skeleton--card" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="skeleton skeleton--btn" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="skeleton skeleton--bubble" aria-hidden="true"></span></div>

Sizes

Sizes follow the content they stand for.

States

One state: shimmering. After 10 s without content the page shows its error state instead.

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

  • Skeletons take the width of what they replace at each breakpoint.

Theming

A gradient from --color-bg-subtle to --color-bg-subtle-hover.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

aria-hidden="true" on each shape; aria-busy="true" on the region.

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not focusable; content is read in order.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The region announces nothing until content arrives.

Contrast

Live ratios, light then dark.

Shape (decorative)

Motion

Shimmer loops at --duration-shimmer; it stops under reduced motion.

Touch

Not a target.

Content

  • No text inside skeletons.
