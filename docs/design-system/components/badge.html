Badge

Badges label a fact with a word and a shape: the confidence of an observation, the status of a metric, the state of a missing-tool request.

Overview

Two families:

  • Confidence (.conf): confirmed (filled dot), likely (half dot), guess (ring), claimed only (dashed ring) and not confirmed (bar: a field that did not follow the console, FR-025; class conf--refused). Used on observations, metrics and controls (FR-055).
  • Status pill (.pill): a missing-tool request’s state, Open · 2 devices (signal) or Built (success).

Colour always comes with the word and the shape (1.4.1).

Anatomy

  1. Shape6px; fill, half, ring, dashed ring or bar.
  2. Word--text-overline, lower case.

Responsive behaviour

  • Never wraps; sits at the end of a row and the row text wraps instead.

Theming

Each confidence reads a status pair: confirmed --color-success-*, likely --color-warning-*, refused --color-danger-*, guess and claimed --color-bg-subtle with muted text. The open pill uses --color-signal-subtle and --color-fg-signal.

Content

  • Observation confidence: confirmed, likely, guess. Metric and control status: confirmed, claimed only, claimed, not seen, and not confirmed when a decoded field did not follow the console within 15 s (FR-025; mocks README, open question 4).
  • Lower case, no full stop.
