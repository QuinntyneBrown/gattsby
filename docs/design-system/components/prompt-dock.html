Prompt dock

The prompt dock is the one thing the agent is waiting on: a 20px instruction, a line of detail and the answers, pinned at the bottom of the screen within reach of the thumb.

Overview

The dock (.dock) holds, top to bottom: the live strip (phones), the prompt title (--text-prompt), detail, and either answer buttons, quick replies, or nothing while a read-only step runs. Under it, the composer lets the person type or speak to the agent. From 992px the dock moves beside the feed.

Anatomy

  1. Prompt title20px semibold: the instruction.
  2. DetailWhat happens next.
  3. AnswersPrimary fills the row; 56px.

States

The dock’s states, each drawn in the mocks:

  • Asking: title, detail, answers.
  • Running: a read-only step runs with no prompt (“Reading the maker and model”, FR-048 AC1).
  • Approval: the dock defers to the approval sheet.
  • Checklist: the same dock with the playbook’s next step and Done / Skip.
  • Checks: an eyebrow counts a short sequence (“Check 1 of 3”) and replies answer each one (FR-014).
  • Recording: the eyebrow carries a Live badge naming the label (“Recording · slow”), a progress bar counts the window, and Skip ends it early (FR-028, FR-051).
  • Checking an effect: after an approved command, the dock asks what the console shows (FR-038).
  • Failed: the title states the problem (“Storage is full”), the detail says what was kept, and the actions recover (NFR-008).

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 768px the primary answer fills the row and the secondary wraps; the dock is pinned at the bottom (NFR-010).
  • From 768px answers size to their labels.
  • From 992px the dock sits in the right column above the side panel; the live strip hides.

Theming

The dock is --color-bg-surface with a top rule; replies and the composer sit on --color-bg-canvas so they read as separate controls.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<section aria-labelledby="prompt-title" aria-live="polite">: a new prompt is announced once.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabAnswers, then the composer.
EnterIn the composer, sends.

Focus

When a new prompt arrives, focus stays where it was; the live region announces it. The composer shows the ring on its pill (:focus-within).

Labelling

The composer input is labelled “Message the agent”; mic and send are named.

Contrast

Live ratios, light then dark.

Prompt title
Detail
Composer placeholder

Motion

None; prompts replace in place.

Touch

Answers are 56px; composer controls 44px inside a 48px pill; 8px between targets.

Content

  • Title: one imperative instruction about the machine (“Raise the incline to 2%”).
  • Detail: what Gattsby will do and what to keep the same.
  • Answers: the observed outcomes.
