Foundation

Content

Plain, calm and specific. The agent says what it will do and why; buttons say what happens; errors say what to do next. The specs’ words, never synonyms.

Overview

The person reading is standing beside a machine in a store or gym, often in noise, sometimes a non-technical helper following the agent. Every string is provisional until the Gattsby: Behavior Description lands in docs/behavior.md (mocks README, open question 1).

Voice

  • The agent speaks in the first person, in short sentences: “I’ll listen for 10 s once you confirm.”
  • Every step has a why: “Why: Most machines talk to one device at a time.”
  • Never blame the person or the device. Say what was seen.
  • Honest about limits: “an encryption layer I can’t see through from a browser. I didn’t try to get around it.”

Numbers, bytes and time

  • Bytes: upper-case hex pairs with spaces, 02 4E 02; UUIDs 0x2ACD.
  • Units with a space: 6.0 km/h, 214 m, 2.2 GB. One decimal for speed and incline.
  • Times: 14:27:15 in the log; “2 h ago”, “Today”, “Nov 22” in lists; “Wed 25 Nov 2026” in full.
  • Counts in words under ten in sentences: “three look-alike neighbours”.

Usage

  • Sentence case everywhere.
  • No exclamation marks; no “please” in instructions to the person beside a machine.
  • Model output is shown as text, as the model wrote it, after validation; never formatted as HTML.
