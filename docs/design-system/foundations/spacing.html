Foundation

Spacing

A 4px scale from 0 to 96px. Components use up to 24px inside; layout uses 24px and more between.

Overview

Every padding, gap and margin is a --space-* token; the number is the multiple of 4px (--space-4 = 16px). --space-0-5 (2px) exists for optical nudges only. The mocks also used 6, 10 and 14px; extraction snapped them up to 8, 12 and 16px (see the drift log).

Inside and between

  • Inside a control: 8–16px (--space-2 to --space-4).
  • Inside a card, sheet or dock: 16–24px.
  • Between related items: 8–12px; between groups 32px (--space-8).
  • Between adjacent touch targets: at least --target-gap (8px).
  • Page margin: --layout-margin, 20px on phones, 32px from 768px.
