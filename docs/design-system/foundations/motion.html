Foundation

Motion

Short, functional motion: 150ms for colour and press, 260ms for sheets. Under reduced motion every transition becomes instant; only status loops (spinners) keep moving.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview
  • Durations
  • Easing
  • Loops
  • Usage
  • Reduced motion

Overview

Motion explains where something came from (a sheet rises from the bottom) or confirms a press. Nothing moves for decoration, and nothing moves on a screen where the person is watching a machine unless it reports what the machine is doing.

Usage

  • Sheets: rise over --duration-slow with --ease-enter on phones; scale in from 576px.
  • Hover and press: --duration-fast.
  • Meters and progress: --duration-deliberate.
  • Never animate a live reading’s value.

Reduced motion

prefers-reduced-motion: reduce sets --duration-fast/base/slow/deliberate to 0.01ms in tokens.css, so components need no media query of their own. components.css also stops the shimmer and the pulse.
