Foundation

Shape

Five radii and full, and two border widths. Radius grows with the size of the thing: 12px controls, 16px cards, 24px sheets.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview
  • Radii
  • Border widths
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

Soft, generous corners from the Magic Patterns designs. Pills (--radius-full) mark things you tap repeatedly or that are status: chips, replies, the composer, icon buttons and the emergency stop.

RadiusUse

xl 24pxSheets
fullChips, replies, icon buttons, composer, emergency stop, meters

Border widths

1px hairlines for edges and rules; 2px for the focus ring, the stage marker and the danger sheet ring.

Usage

  • Nested shapes take a smaller radius than their container.
  • Never square a control that is a pill elsewhere.
