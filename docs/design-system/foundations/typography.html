Foundation

Typography

Geist for words, Geist Mono for bytes, UUIDs and numbers. Body stays 16px; the agent’s prompt is 20px and live readings 32px so they read at arm’s length beside a machine.

WCAG 2.2 AA

Overview

Two variable fonts, bundled and self-hosted (SIL Open Font License, assets/fonts/): Geist and Geist Mono. Nothing loads from a CDN (AGENTS.md, NFR-012). Headings are semibold with tight tracking; numbers in tables and readings are tabular.

Families

Geist — the quick device

Geist Mono 0x2ACD 02 4E 02

Sans for words, mono for anything a person compares character by character.

Scale and roles

Fifteen role shorthands (font: var(--text-…)), each a weight, size, line height and family. Use roles, not sizes.

Usage

  • One <h1> per screen: the app bar title or the page title.
  • Prompts use --text-prompt; never smaller than 20px (NFR-009).
  • Live values use --text-metric on phones; 17px mono in the side panel.
  • Bytes, UUIDs, versions, times and sizes in mono with font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums.
  • Sentence case everywhere; overlines are the only uppercase, and confidence words stay lower case.
  • Prose stops at --layout-measure (62ch).
  • Nothing below 12px.

Responsive type

Sizes are fixed steps in rem, so they follow the person’s browser text size. Two roles step up at 768px: the home hero (30 → 36px) and the verdict title (30 → 42px).

Accessibility

  • 16px minimum for inputs so mobile browsers do not zoom.
  • Line heights 1.5 for body and 1.6 for the agent’s messages; layouts survive WCAG 1.4.12 text spacing.
  • The wordmark’s tight tracking is for the logo only.
