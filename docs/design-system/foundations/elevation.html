Foundation

Elevation

Four shadow levels and a focus shadow, a surface hierarchy that also works in ink, and nine named layers.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview
  • Shadows
  • Surface hierarchy
  • Z-order
  • Usage

Overview

Gattsby is mostly flat: cards sit on paper with a hairline and --shadow-1. Sheets use --shadow-4, cast upward because they rise from the bottom. In ink the shadows are darker and stronger, and raised surfaces are lighter than the canvas, because a shadow alone barely shows on a dark page.

Usage

  • One level of elevation per surface; do not stack shadows.
  • In ink rely on the surface step, not the shadow, to show what is raised.
